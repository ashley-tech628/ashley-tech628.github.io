01 / PROBLEM

What needed to work.

High reconstruction fidelity can hide a weak generative prior. A chest-radiograph model must preserve spatial structure while generating new samples without using an input image, and the two behaviors need separate evaluation.

02 / APPROACH

The engineering behind it.

Factor the representation into a 128-dimensional global Gaussian and an 8×32×32 spatial latent conditioned on it. Optimize a hybrid pixel/SSIM/Sobel objective with KL warm-up, then freeze the encoder and priors while adapting only the decoder with a weak, gradient-balanced patch-discriminator loss.

  1. 01

    Residual spatial posterior: learn corrections to conditional-prior mean and log variance, with bounded variance and no deterministic encoder-to-decoder skips.

  2. 02

    Inject local stochastic features at the 32×32 decoder stage through a bounded learned gain; keep posterior reconstruction distinct from prior-only generation.

  3. 03

    H3B uses a 0.35 local KL multiplier, ten-epoch warm-up, AdamW, gradient clipping and EMA; checkpoint selection fixes the final KL weight.

  4. 04

    H6A freezes encoder/global map/prior parameters, verifies the source-checkpoint hash, and pairs global/local RNG streams for a common 522-sample generation evaluation.

TECHNICAL DEEP DIVE / GENERATIVE MODELING

Two latent levels. Two evaluation questions.

Conditional spatial priors and residual posterior inference

The hierarchical posterior factorizes as q(zg | x)q(zℓ | x, zg). The generative path uses p(zg)p(zℓ | zg), with a 128-dimensional standard-normal global prior and an 8×32×32 conditional spatial Gaussian. The posterior predicts residual corrections to the prior mean and log variance. Only sampled latent variables reach the decoder; encoder features parameterize the posterior rather than bypassing the bottleneck.

Residual convolutional blocks use GroupNorm and SiLU. A learned global map provides 192-channel context at 8×8, while the local code is projected into the 32×32 decoder stage through a bounded tanh gain. Bilinear upsampling and convolution produce the final 128×128 grayscale image.

Preserve structure while controlling the information bottleneck

H3B combines batch-averaged summed pixel MSE, 48×SSIM penalty, 32×Sobel-edge L1, global KL and 0.35×spatial KL. A ten-epoch warm-up reaches the final KL weight of 1. AdamW starts at 1.5×10−4, with batch size 16, clipping at 1.0 and EMA decay 0.999. Checkpoint selection evaluates at the final KL weight even during warm-up, keeping the selection objective comparable across epochs.

MSEsum + 48(1 − SSIM) + 32LSobel + β(KLg + 0.35KLℓ)

The reported per-pixel MSE has a different normalization from the optimization term. Structural penalties and nonuniform KL weighting make this a hybrid VAE objective, rather than an exact negative ELBO with a specified image likelihood.

Adapt the decoder while preserving latent distributions

H6A freezes the H3B encoder, global mapping and both prior/posterior parameter networks. A spectral-normalized patch discriminator uses hinge loss on real images, reconstructions and prior samples. Discriminator updates detach generated images; decoder updates freeze discriminator parameters while retaining gradients through its operations.

The adversarial coefficient is detached and scaled using reconstruction/adversarial gradient norms at the final decoder convolution, targeting a ratio of 0.05 with a cap of 20. Decoder/discriminator learning rates are 10−5 and 5×10−5; the refinement lasts 15 epochs. Source-checkpoint SHA-256 and split-manifest checks keep the adaptation tied to the intended H3B run.

Evaluate the model through its actual sampling path

The paired H6A evaluator scores both models in one routine, resetting separate global and spatial RNG streams and matching KID subset sampling. Reconstruction uses posterior means; generation draws independently from the priors. Real grayscale references and generated images are converted to three-channel uint8 inputs for ImageNet Inception features. A 6.38-point FID decrease is an observed small-validation improvement, with no established statistical significance.

The data loader splits the original 5,216 training images by class into 4,694 training and 522 validation images. Labels stratify the split but do not condition generation. Patient-disjoint splitting was not established, and the supplied test directory was not evaluated in the report.

Implementation verification and research boundaries

Dataset-free CPU checks verify hybrid-loss gradients, encoder-free prior generation, frozen-parameter invariance during a decoder update, weight reload, and stable/disjoint image-level splits. These checks validate implementation behavior, not the historical FID or SSIM measurements. The public repository preserves the original report, source and metric provenance; no clinical effectiveness or held-out test performance is claimed.

04 / SCOPE & TRADEOFFS

Know what the result means.

Report-backed historical validation results; trained checkpoints and complete raw evaluation logs are not in the source package. Image-level stratification does not establish patient-disjoint validation. ImageNet FID/KID/IS are not clinical measures, no held-out test score is reported, and the small FID change has no established statistical significance.
