TECHNICAL DEEP DIVE

From implementation to evidence.

Two distinct generation problems

The causal language model generates symbolic SATB sequences with weight tying, relative-position bias and periodic beat embeddings. The sequence-to-sequence harmonizer encodes the full provided melody while keeping the harmony decoder causal. Separate melody and harmony vocabularies, train-only transposition augmentation and eighth-note grids define the data representation.

Sampling and musical diagnostics

Sampling and reranking examine repetition, pitch distribution, consonance, voice crossing and n-gram overlap. Unigram, bigram, lookup and untrained baselines expose failure modes. These heuristics do not guarantee musical correctness, and teacher-forced token accuracy must remain separate from listening quality or memorization claims.

04 / SCOPE & TRADEOFFS

Know what the result means.

Historical baseline scoring masks differ from the Transformer evaluation. Teacher-forced token accuracy does not establish listener preference or free-running musical quality.
