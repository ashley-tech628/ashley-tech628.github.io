GENERATIVE MODELING / ECE 285 RESEARCH

Hierarchical Chest X-ray Generation

Anatomical structure versus generative fidelity.

A conditional latent hierarchy learns coarse anatomy and spatial variation. The study compares posterior reconstruction with independent prior generation, then examines a controlled decoder-only refinement.

01 / MODEL ARCHITECTURE

Global and conditional spatial latents.

The generative model factorizes as p(zg)p(zℓ | zg)p(x | zg, zℓ). Reconstruction adds amortized global and local posterior inference.

128-D global representation

A residual encoder produces an 8×8 feature map. Global pooling and a linear head parameterize the Gaussian posterior; a learned projection maps zg back to 192×8×8 context.

8×32×32 conditional local code

The local posterior combines 80-channel encoder features at 32×32 with 96-channel prior context. It predicts corrections to the prior mean and log variance.

Bounded spatial injection

A learned projection of zℓ, scaled by tanh(a), enters the decoder at 32×32. Final upsampling yields a grayscale sigmoid output. No deterministic encoder–decoder skips are used.

02 / TRAINING DESIGN

Learn the hierarchy. Refine the decoder.

STAGE A / H3B

Structure-aware variational training

Pixel MSE is combined with SSIM and Sobel-edge alignment. The KL multiplier warms up over ten epochs; local KL is weighted by 0.35.

STAGE B / H6A

Restricted adversarial adaptation

The encoder, global mapping and latent distributions remain frozen. A spectral-normalized patch critic scores real images, reconstructions and independent prior samples.

04 / FINDINGS & SCOPE

Structure improves before texture becomes convincing.

  1. Spatial capacity needs appropriate regularization.

    H3A enlarges the local grid but has worse reported reconstruction than H2. H3B changes the local KL weighting and reaches the lowest listed MSE. H3C gives a marginally higher SSIM with slightly higher MSE.

  2. A better reconstruction does not establish a better prior.

    Posterior evaluation uses an input image; independent generation uses only the learned priors. The paired H6A evaluator resets global/local latent streams and KID subset sampling to compare the same generation protocol.

  3. The refinement gain remains modest.

    Matched FID/KID/IS favor H6A, while samples remain texture-deficient. One seed, 522 validation images and ImageNet features do not establish statistical significance or radiological validity. Patient-disjoint splitting was not established.
