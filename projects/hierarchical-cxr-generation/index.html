GENERATIVE MODELING / ECE 285 RESEARCH

Hierarchical Chest X-ray Generation

Hierarchical synthesis. Protocol-aware evaluation.

A conditional latent hierarchy learns coarse anatomy and spatial variation. The study compares posterior reconstruction with independent prior generation, then examines decoder-only refinement over three sampling seeds.

01 / MODEL ARCHITECTURE

Global and conditional spatial latents.

The generative model factorizes as p(zg)p(zℓ | zg)p(x | zg, zℓ). Reconstruction adds amortized global and local posterior inference.

128-D global representation

A residual encoder produces an 8×8 feature map. Global pooling and a linear head parameterize the Gaussian posterior; a learned projection maps zg back to 192×8×8 context.

8×32×32 conditional local code

The local posterior combines 80-channel encoder features at 32×32 with 96-channel prior context. It predicts corrections to the prior mean and log variance.

Bounded spatial injection

A learned projection of zℓ, scaled by tanh(a), enters the decoder at 32×32. Final upsampling yields a grayscale sigmoid output. No deterministic encoder–decoder skips are used.

Structure-aware variational training

Pixel MSE is combined with SSIM and Sobel-edge alignment. The KL multiplier increases from 0.1 to 1 over ten epochs; local KL is weighted by 0.35.

04 / FINDINGS & SCOPE

A modest generation gain, with reconstruction held in view.

  1. Feature space is part of the result.

    Ignite default FID uses 1,000-D classifier logits; the separate pooled-2048 evaluation operates on a different representation. Each comparison uses a matched preprocessing protocol, and their absolute scores must remain separate.

  2. Generation improves without a reconstruction gain.

    H6A lowers Ignite FID for all three sampling seeds, while mean IS is nearly unchanged and paired reconstruction MSE increases slightly. Posterior fidelity and unconditional synthesis measure different behavior.

  3. Sampling consistency has a limited scope.

    Three sampling seeds reuse one fixed validation set and do not establish statistical significance. Thoracic shapes are recognizable, but ribs and pulmonary markings remain blurred. ImageNet metrics are not clinical validation; patient-disjoint splitting was not established.
