RevProof

Update documents from evidence, one claim at a time.

An evidence-grounded editor for evolving documents: select supporting evidence, locate an obsolete span, generate a short replacement, and copy the rest of the article. A paired FRUIT-WIKI development study improved full-article exact match from 9/100 to 20/100 over the preceding pipeline.

01 / PROBLEM

What needed to work.

A newly released fact can contradict a sentence in an older document. A full rewrite risks altering unrelated claims; a local update must identify the right evidence and edit span before changing the text.

02 / APPROACH

The engineering behind it.

Rank sentence–evidence pairs, propose character spans with a QA locator, infill a replacement using a LoRA-adapted FLAN-T5-XL encoder–decoder, and render the article by copying every character outside the selected span. Candidate checks can reject unsupported new numbers or abstain.

  1. 01

    Factor evidence selection, span localization and replacement generation so each error can be measured separately.

  2. 02

    Train the generator on short evidence-supported edits and preserve the unaffected article with a deterministic renderer.

  3. 03

    Compare V1, V2 forced editing and a fixed abstention gate on the same 100 silver development articles.

  4. 04

    Audit nonexact outputs for complete semantic updates and partial recovery of the primary new fact.

TECHNICAL DEEP DIVE

From implementation to evidence.

Local generator and constrained rendering

A RoBERTa sentence–evidence selector scores candidate pairs, a learned QA model proposes character spans, and a LoRA-adapted FLAN-T5-XL encoder–decoder predicts a replacement phrase from the source context and cited evidence. The renderer copies every character outside the chosen span; candidate checks can reject newly introduced unsupported numbers.

Paired evaluation and manual review

V1 and V2 were compared on the same 100 FRUIT-WIKI silver development articles. V2 forced editing produced 20 full-article exact matches versus V1's 9; a fixed gate retained 17 exact matches while changing 69 articles. A retrospective one-reviewer audit found 38 complete semantic updates and 50 primary-fact hits, including partial revisions.

What the failed variants taught us

Boundary reranking, whole-sentence generation, structured copy markers, wider windows and additional V2 training did not exceed V2 in their paired inspected screens. Span selection and evidence-field binding remain more important than simply generating a longer output. Numeric edits were 0/15 exact in the main V2 split.

04 / SCOPE & TRADEOFFS

Know what the result means.

These are development results on 100 FRUIT-WIKI silver examples that have since been inspected. The 38/100 semantic count is a retrospective one-reviewer audit; silver exact match does not establish factual support. Numeric edits remained 0/15 exact, and the proposed version-aware retrieval improvement has not yet been measured.
