TECHNICAL DEEP DIVE

From implementation to evidence.

Feature-level spatiotemporal fusion

A Dense 3D encoder uses four dense blocks (4, 8, 12, 8), growth rate 32 and 64 initial features. A parallel LipNet-style branch applies three spatiotemporal convolution stages. Spatially aligned maps are concatenated before global spatial pooling, then projected with a learned 1×1×1 convolution. Two-layer bidirectional GRU/LSTM modeling follows the fused temporal sequence.

Sequence masking and evaluation

Explicit lengths, packed recurrence and masked temporal attention keep padded frames out of the prediction. The maintained pipeline supports AdamW, cosine decay, class weighting and gradient clipping. Validation balanced accuracy selects checkpoints; test reporting includes macro F1, top-3 accuracy, calibration and robustness probes. The figures below preserve the thesis experiments rather than reporting a fresh thesis-scale training run.

04 / SCOPE & TRADEOFFS

Know what the result means.

This is visual-only, closed-vocabulary word recognition, not sentence transcription. The thesis reports a substantial validation/test generalization gap; model rows do not share a confirmed matched test protocol.
