WikiArt Painting Synthesis

Two GAN approaches to full-dataset painting synthesis.

A full-dataset painting synthesis study: an independently implemented style-conditional GAN trained from scratch, alongside 1024px StyleGAN2-ADA fine-tuning from a credited public WikiArt checkpoint. The high-resolution model generates recognizable subjects, but matched before-and-after evaluation shows no FID gain from additional fine-tuning.

01 / PROBLEM

What needed to work.

Training on a large, visually diverse painting collection is harder than reproducing colors and brush texture: the generator must also retain coherent subjects. The project tests a from-scratch conditional model alongside a higher-resolution transfer model and checks whether additional full-data training actually improves the latter.

02 / APPROACH

The engineering behind it.

Build a 128px style-conditional generator with conditional batch normalization and a spectrally normalized projection discriminator; train it with hinge loss and generator EMA. Separately initialize NVIDIA StyleGAN2-ADA from Peter Baylies's publicly released unconditional WikiArt weights, fine-tune on the assigned collection at native 1024px, and evaluate before and after transfer using the same 1,000-image test protocol.

  1. 01

    Index all 81,444 assigned paintings across 27 style folders. The scratch experiment trains on 73,300 images for 40 epochs; the transfer experiment uses 73,299 training, 4,072 validation, and 4,073 test images.

  2. 02

    Condition only the independently implemented GAN on style labels. Keep the public StyleGAN2 transfer experiment unconditional and credit its WikiArt-pretrained starting weights.

  3. 03

    Use a fixed validation metric to select the fine-tuned EMA checkpoint at 79.459 kimg. The transfer run reaches 80.003 kimg and traverses its training partition once.

  4. 04

    Compare the public and fine-tuned StyleGAN2 generators with identical test images, 1,000 generated samples, seed, truncation, and Inception preprocessing; retain fixed-seed grids and training curves.

TECHNICAL DEEP DIVE

Two models, one complete collection.

Independently implemented conditional GAN

The 128 × 128 generator maps a 128-dimensional noise vector through transposed convolutions. Style embeddings modulate each stage through conditional batch normalization. A spectrally normalized convolutional discriminator combines an image score with a class-projection term. Hinge adversarial loss and generator EMA support training from random initialization. The completed run has 40 epochs and 45,800 optimization steps.

High-resolution transfer model

The separate 1024 × 1024 model uses NVIDIA StyleGAN2-ADA initialized from Peter Baylies's public, already WikiArt-trained checkpoint. It is unconditional. Adaptive augmentation, R1 and path-length regularization, and generator EMA are used during fine-tuning. Validation selected the 79.459-kimg checkpoint; the run reached 80.003 kimg and saw every image in its training partition.

04 / SCOPE & TRADEOFFS

Know what the result means.

The public StyleGAN2 weights were already trained on WikiArt, and their original image list is unavailable. Overlap with this project's validation or test images cannot be ruled out; the scores do not establish generalization to unseen pretraining data. The scratch and transfer models differ in resolution, conditioning, and data split, so their FID/IS rows are not a controlled architecture comparison. These metrics are finite-sample ImageNet-feature estimates, not a complete measure of painterly quality or originality.
