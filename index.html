01 / CONFERENCE PAPER · MLAAI 2023

Java-based Development of a Vehicle Speed Recommendation Model for Assisted Driving

Xinying Liu (Ashley Liu), Hongjie Tao · MLAAI 2023 · Paper ID MLAAI-2368

Investigates recommended vehicle speeds when approaching a signalized intersection. The model combines intersection conditions, traffic congestion and weather-adjusted driver reaction time, implemented and evaluated with simulated traffic data in a Java backend.

The approach models three driving phases—reaction, acceleration or deceleration, and steady-speed travel—within a 115 m approach zone. It derives a feasible recommendation range from the remaining distance and signal timing, then accounts for temperature, humidity and wind conditions through reaction-time assumptions.

Vision-Guided Autonomous Smart Car

Embedded track perception, track-aware guidance and closed-loop vehicle control on an Infineon TC264D. An MT9V03X grayscale camera provides road geometry for steering, while encoder feedback supports motor control.

03 / OUTSTANDING GRADUATION PROJECT · 2023–2024

Visual Lipreading

A Multimodal Lip Recognition Approach Based on Vision and Deep Learning

Independent bachelor thesis investigating closed-vocabulary word recognition from mouth-video sequences. Dense 3D and LipNet-style branches combine complementary spatiotemporal features, followed by a two-layer bidirectional recurrent backend.

The optimized fusion model reports approximately 46.73% validation recognition. The original thesis also shows a substantial validation/test gap; the project case preserves these definitions and the original experimental curves. The executable system uses visual input and predicts words, rather than full-sentence transcription.
